## Geração inicial

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a high resolution portrait 4:5 full-bleed photographic surreal editorial image for an Instagram carousel cover aimed at Brazilian digital marketing agency owners. Primary request: a contemporary marketing agency owner as a witty, imaginative reinterpretation of Atlas carrying the world on his back; instead of Earth, he supports an enormous compact globe-shaped cluster of his work: a miniature modern agency office, laptop, camera for content production, monitors with abstract ads charts and a marketing funnel, and recognizable square Instagram and Meta icon tiles integrated as physical objects in the cluster. One adult man in his thirties, contemporary navy shirt with rolled sleeves and trousers, realistic human anatomy, natural textured face, both arms raised naturally supporting the load at his shoulders. Strong iconic Atlas pose, determined concentrated expression, not pain. The man and load must be BIG, immediately legible, not a small figure lost in a landscape. Scale the massive workload across most of the image width and the upper 62 percent; the man's face and raised arms are clearly visible in the middle-upper section. He is a real person, not a marble statue. Style: premium photorealistic advertising photography of an impossible but physically convincing scene, playful satire, sharp detail, realistic cloth, metal and glass; NOT cartoon, not clay, not flat graphic. Backdrop: luminous warm sand studio atmosphere fading subtly toward deep navy near the lower edge; clear bright upper half, controlled gold accents. Full scene continues behind the lower section, with the lower 35 percent relatively uncluttered for a large headline to be typeset later. No in-image headline, no letters, no page numbers, no border, no watermark. Instagram and Meta symbols can appear on a few square physical tiles; keep other screens abstract without readable text. Do not add medical equipment or unrelated fantasy props. Make the central visual striking and large.

## Edição final: expressão de exaustão

Edit this exact image. Change ONLY the man's facial expression and a small natural downward tilt of his head. He must look EXHAUSTED from carrying all the work, not heroic or triumphant: lowered tired gaze, heavy eyelids, relaxed fatigued brows, subtly open mouth as if exhaling, believable tired face. Remove the upward hopeful gaze and confident hero expression. Keep him awake and working, visibly drained but no exaggerated grimace and no injury. Preserve the same face identity, body pose, hands, clothing, enormous agency workload, Instagram and Meta tiles, objects, lighting, colors, camera angle, composition, image dimensions and all background details. Photorealistic natural expression. No added text.
